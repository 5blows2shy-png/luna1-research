# Klyro advertisement scripts

All figures shown are fictional Harbor Supply Co. demonstration data. Forecasts and safe-to-spend values are estimates, not guarantees.

## 30-second cutdown

Your bank balance is not the same as what your business can safely spend. Klyro looks at upcoming obligations to estimate what may actually be available. See who owes you money, what is coming due, and how much cash is tied up in inventory. Before a major purchase, see how it may affect your runway. Already use QuickBooks? Keep it. Klyro. Know your numbers. Know your next move.

## 15-second cutdown

Your bank balance is not the whole story. Klyro helps you see upcoming bills, customer payments, cash tied up in inventory, and what may be safe to spend. Know your numbers. Know your next move.

## Recording instructions

1. Run the application and open `/klyro` at a 1920×1080 browser viewport.
2. Playback starts automatically, runs for exactly 60 seconds across seven scenes, and loops continuously.
3. The website presentation intentionally includes no playback or recording controls.
5. Record the 16:9 master first. Critical copy, figures, and CTA are center-safe for later 1:1 and 9:16 crops.
6. Browser or operating-system reduced-motion preferences disable scene and number transitions while keeping every scene navigable.

The route makes no network requests, contains no forms or payment actions, and does not connect to QuickBooks. The connected status is fictional display data only.
