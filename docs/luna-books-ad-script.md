# Klyro advertisement scripts

All figures and files shown are fictional Harbor Supply Co. demonstration data. Klyro is in development. Automated classifications, forecasts, narratives, and journal entries remain review-only and require appropriate accounting or management approval.

## Primary 60-second version

| Time | Scene | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | The hook | Your bank balance might say forty-two thousand dollars. But how much can your business actually spend? |
| 5–13s | Bring in the books | Klyro brings your financial files into one review workspace—across CSV, PDF, Excel, and OpenDocument formats—with every row linked to its source. |
| 13–21s | Decision Board | Start with the question that matters. Klyro’s Decision Board prioritizes what deserves attention without changing the underlying accounting. |
| 21–30s | Clean and prepare | Clean transactions, review duplicates, and turn activity into balanced journal-entry suggestions for accountant approval. |
| 30–40s | Cash and financials | See cash-flow intelligence, financial-statement previews, and a conditional thirteen-week outlook—so today’s numbers provide context for what may come next. |
| 40–50s | Close and report | Then build a Monthly Close Board Packet with a detailed summary, board narrative, review items, source coverage, and complete Excel or PDF exports. |
| 50–60s | Brand and CTA | From source files to better-informed next moves. Klyro. Import. Review. Decide. Close. Know your numbers. Know your next move. |

## 30-second cutdown

Your bank balance is not the whole story. Klyro brings CSV, PDF, Excel, and OpenDocument files into one traceable review workspace. Choose the decision that matters, clean transactions, review duplicates, prepare journal-entry suggestions, and understand your cash outlook. Then turn the month into a detailed board packet with accountant review and Excel or PDF exports. Klyro. Know your numbers. Know your next move.

## 15-second cutdown

Bring your financial files together. Clean transactions, surface review items, understand cash, and build a clearer monthly close packet. Klyro turns business records into better-informed next moves.

## Recording instructions

1. Run the application and open the advertisement surface at a 1920×1080 browser viewport.
2. Playback starts automatically, runs for exactly 60 seconds across seven scenes, and loops continuously.
3. The presentation intentionally includes no playback or recording controls.
4. Record the 16:9 master first. Critical copy, figures, and CTA are center-safe for later 1:1 and 9:16 crops.
5. Browser or operating-system reduced-motion preferences disable scene and number transitions while preserving the complete sequence.

The advertisement makes no network requests, uploads no files, initiates no external actions, and performs no accounting or financial writes. Every shown file, transaction, calculation, forecast, and export is fictional demonstration content.
