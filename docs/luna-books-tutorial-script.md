# Luna Books tutorial voiceover

Target runtime: approximately 3 minutes 40 seconds. All businesses, people, accounts, transactions, and amounts shown are fictional demonstration data.

## Scene 1 — Welcome (17 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Open the demo dashboard and pause on the summary cards.
- **Voiceover:** “Welcome to Luna Books. Your books tell you what happened. Luna helps you understand what needs attention next. This fictional Harbor Supply workspace brings transactions, cash, invoices, inventory, and decisions into one review-first view.”
- **Important UI element:** Safe demo data badge and safe-to-spend card.
- **Transition cue:** Select Business Profile.

## Scene 2 — Business Profile (16 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Scan the business details and private workspace notice.
- **Voiceover:** “Each business receives its own private Luna Books workspace. Here, Harbor Supply uses accrual accounting with three connected demo accounts and two team members. Access stays tied to a specific business and role.”
- **Important UI element:** Business profile fields and private workspace note.
- **Transition cue:** Select Transactions.

## Scene 3 — Transactions (22 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Pause on Coastal Office Supply, then its explanation.
- **Voiceover:** “Imported transactions become an organized review queue. Luna can suggest categories, flag possible duplicates and unusual amounts, and show receipt matches. Recorded facts, calculations, and recommendations are labeled separately. This category suggestion is based on vendor history and prior approved transactions.”
- **Important UI element:** Highlighted transaction and evidence labels.
- **Transition cue:** Select Cash Position.

## Scene 4 — Cash Position (20 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Follow the equation, then review obligations.
- **Voiceover:** “A bank balance is not the same as safe-to-spend cash. Harbor Supply has forty-two thousand eight hundred fifty dollars in the bank. After eighteen thousand three hundred dollars of known obligations, Luna estimates twenty-four thousand five hundred fifty dollars is safe to spend.”
- **Important UI element:** Cash equation and included obligations.
- **Transition cue:** Select Cash Runway.

## Scene 5 — Cash Runway (18 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Hold on 6.4 weeks, then scan the inputs.
- **Voiceover:** “Luna estimates a cash runway of 6.4 weeks from current cash, bills, payroll, recurring costs, expected collections, and inventory plans. This is an estimate based on today’s information—not a guaranteed outcome.”
- **Important UI element:** Runway number, preferred reserve, and inputs.
- **Transition cue:** Select Receivables.

## Scene 6 — Receivables (20 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Highlight Seaport Café and the recommendation evidence.
- **Voiceover:** “Three invoices totaling fourteen thousand six hundred dollars are overdue. Luna highlights Seaport Café first because it is the oldest and largest balance. The recommendation includes its reason, evidence, estimated impact, urgency, and confidence. Nothing is sent automatically.”
- **Important UI element:** Selected invoice and recommendation card.
- **Transition cue:** Select Bills.

## Scene 7 — Bills (16 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Show the $12,400 supplier bill and disabled payment button.
- **Voiceover:** “A twelve-thousand-four-hundred-dollar supplier payment is due next week. It is already included in Luna’s cash outlook, so the forecast does not ignore a known obligation. Payments are disabled in this demonstration.”
- **Important UI element:** Included status and disabled action.
- **Transition cue:** Select Inventory.

## Scene 8 — Inventory (21 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Scan the inventory bar, recoverable cash, and Product B detail.
- **Voiceover:** “Harbor Supply has eighty-two thousand five hundred dollars at cost tied up in inventory. Luna separates healthy, slow-moving, dead, and excess stock and estimates twelve thousand one hundred dollars may be recoverable. Product B is slow-moving because only four units sold in ninety days while thirty-eight remain.”
- **Important UI element:** Inventory composition and classification evidence.
- **Transition cue:** Select Can I Afford This?

## Scene 9 — Can I Afford This? (22 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Compare cash and runway before and after.
- **Voiceover:** “Before committing twelve thousand dollars to inventory, Can I Afford This shows the estimated effect. Cash falls to thirty thousand eight hundred fifty dollars, and runway falls from 6.4 to 4.8 weeks. Luna marks the decision caution because it moves below the preferred reserve.”
- **Important UI element:** Before-and-after values and caution result.
- **Transition cue:** Select 13-Week Forecast.

## Scene 10 — 13-Week Forecast (22 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Show Expected, briefly select Conservative, then return to Expected.
- **Voiceover:** “The thirteen-week forecast combines starting cash, customer receipts, operating costs, payroll, supplier payments, and planned inventory purchases. Expected is the recording default, while conservative and optimistic views make assumptions visible. Forecasts depend on timing and are not guarantees.”
- **Important UI element:** Scenario control and cash chart.
- **Transition cue:** Select Recommended Actions.

## Scene 11 — Recommended Actions (22 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Move across the three recommendation cards.
- **Voiceover:** “Luna’s top three decisions are to collect the oldest invoice, delay a slow-moving reorder, and reorder a product approaching stockout. Every suggestion shows why it matters, the supporting evidence, estimated impact, urgency, and confidence. The financial numbers come from structured demo data—not an AI guess.”
- **Important UI element:** Three traceable recommendation cards.
- **Transition cue:** Select Reports & Access.

## Scene 12 — Reports, QuickBooks, and Accountant Access (24 seconds)

- **Screen action:** Scan reports, connection status, invitation preview, and final lockup.
- **Voiceover:** “Luna keeps the Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, and Financial Health Report close at hand. QuickBooks connection status is visible, and accountant access can be previewed by permission. In this demo, no token is loaded and no invitation can be sent. Know your numbers. Know what needs attention. Know your next move. Run and understand your business.”
- **Important UI element:** Reports, connection protections, and final call to action.
- **Transition cue:** Hold the final panel for five seconds, then end recording.
